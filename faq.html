Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about CodeCraft Academy.

On the Login page, click the "Forgot password?" link. Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a password reset link directly to your inbox. If you don't see the email, please check your spam folder.

After a successful payment, you will be automatically redirected to your Dashboard. Navigate to the "My Courses" section, click on the course you purchased, and click the "Join Telegram Group" button to access the course materials and community.

Due to the digital nature of our courses, all purchases are final. However, if you experience severe technical issues that prevent you from accessing the course, please contact our support team within 7 days of purchase, and we will review your case.

During the Sign Up process, select "Instructor — creating courses & content" as your role. Once logged in, go to your Dashboard and click "Manage Courses". From there, you can create new courses and bundles for approval by the Admin.

Every user gets a unique referral link. When you share this link with a friend and they sign up and make their first purchase, you earn a cash commission. You can view your total earnings and stats on the "Affiliate Program" page.

Once you reach a minimum balance of ₦5,000, you can request a real cash payout directly to your bank account by filling out the payout form on the same Affiliate page!

You can reach us via our "Contact Us" page in the menu. If you need immediate assistance with a technical or payment issue, click the floating bug icon at the bottom-left corner of your screen to open the Support Ticket page.
